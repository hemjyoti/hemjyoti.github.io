excerpt: 'In this work, we demonstrate a swing-up and grasping maneuver for the suspended-aerial manipulation platform. The swing-up motion of the platform is envisioned for target applications where its propulsion units cannot generate sufficient thrust to reach a desired pose. An energy regulation approach is employed to drive the suspended aerial manipulator platform to the desired limit cycle. Once the periodic oscillations are achieved, the designed online motion generator commands the manipulator to approach and grasp the given target. In this work, we employ a control strategy in which the controllers for the aerial platform and the manipulator are designed independently, while considering the dynamic coupling between the two subsystems. Our proposed approach relies on onboard sensors and a camera-tracking system, and is validated through extensive simulation studies and experimental tests.'
